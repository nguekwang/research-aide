# What may be published

Public repositories and sites can be read by anyone, and private ones may become public later.

- Publish only personal information the owner has explicitly approved. Before committing, check the diff: no phone number, address, full birth date, private email addresses or secret values.
- Do not present other people's work (projects, fonts, writing, constructed languages, blogs) as the owner's, and do not name them in a repository without the owner's request.
- Do not commit fonts or other files the owner has no right to redistribute, such as commercial fonts or fonts made by someone else.
- Affiliation: do not use titles that look like an official position (such as "assistant"). Do not name supervising or collaborating professors until they have agreed.
- Data that must stay private, such as measurement results and plans, stays out of public repositories.
- A public repository never links to a private one.
